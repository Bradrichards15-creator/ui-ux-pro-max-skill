**[Markup example card]**
**[Label]** Mobilisation - Response as submitted
**[Body text with 5 highlighted phrases, footnoted below]**
If successful, we intend to mobilise the contract over 12-14 weeks(1). We will use a job tracker(2) to plan and manage onboarding, and are willing to appoint a named contract manager(3) who will follow the KPIs(4) set out in the specification. We will also deliver community benefits(5) throughout the contract term.

**[Footnote 1]** "intend to" isn't selling it to the panel, and it still misses the 10-week spec. Two problems, one sentence.
**[Footnote 2]** which tracker? Who updates it, and how do we see it?
**[Footnote 3]** "willing to" isn't a commitment. Will you, or won't you?
**[Footnote 4]** "follow" isn't "report." Which KPIs, measured how, shared how often?
**[Footnote 5]** what benefits, worth how much, delivered when, proven how?

**[Note under card]** every sentence is a promise to maybe do something.
