Happy with your suppliers?

Spend Help isn't only about cutting costs - sometimes the numbers are fine, and it's the process around them that's costing you time. If that's where you're at, here's some housekeeping worth doing anyway.

Renewal tracking

Contracts that auto-renew are where money quietly leaks, whether the rate's good or not. I'll help you set up a simple way to flag renewal dates before they catch you out.

Process & templates

No formal procurement function? I'll help you put a lightweight process in place - supplier onboarding, contract tracking, simple approval steps - so buying decisions aren't ad hoc.

Sourcing strategy

Next time you do go to market, there's a right way to approach it. I'll help you think through category strategy and supplier selection - so you're not just picking the first quote that lands.
