Mobilisation — Response as submitted
If successful, we intend to mobilise the contract over 12–14 weeks1. We will use a job tracker2 to plan and manage onboarding, and are willing to appoint a named contract manager3 who will follow the KPIs4 set out in the specification. We will also deliver community benefits5 throughout the contract term.
1 — "intend to" isn't selling it to the panel, and it still misses the 10-week spec. Two problems, one sentence.
2 — which tracker? Who updates it, and how do we see it?
3 — "willing to" isn't a commitment. Will you, or won't you?
4 — "follow" isn't "report." Which KPIs, measured how, shared how often?
5 — what benefits, worth how much, delivered when, proven how?
every sentence is a promise to maybe do something.
