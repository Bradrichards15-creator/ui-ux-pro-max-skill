Frequently asked questions

How much does bid support cost?+

It depends on what you need - a bid review and mock scoring is £110, response writing is £70 per question. Full pricing is on the Tenders page.

How long does a bid review take?+

Bid reviews and mock scoring can usually be turned around the same day. Full response writing is normally a two-day turnaround, though it varies depending on how busy things are - let me know your deadline and I'll tell you honestly whether it's workable.

What makes Procurement Helps different from an agency?+

No agency mark-up, no account managers, no templates. It's just me, and every response is written specifically for your business and that tender.

Do you only work with public sector bids?+

Yes, public sector tenders are the main focus. They follow a fair, regulated process, so every supplier gets a genuine chance - a well-written, tidy submission is what makes the difference.
