Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 August 2026 · Version 1.1

Procurement Helps ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This notice explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data when you visit procurementhelps.com or engage our services.

1. Who we are

Procurement Helps is a sole trading consultancy providing bid support and procurement cost-reduction advice. You can contact us at brad@procurementhelps.com.

We are not currently required to register with the ICO as a Data Controller (registration thresholds are based on turnover and processing activity); however, we comply with UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 principles in how we handle your data.

2. What data we collect and why

Contact form

When you submit an enquiry via our website contact form, we collect your name, email address, company name (if provided) and enquiry details.

Why: To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote or initial consultation.
Legal basis: Your consent (you chose to submit the form).
Retention: We keep this data for 3 years from the date of your enquiry, or until the engagement ends + 1 year, whichever is longer. After that, we delete it.

Website analytics

Where enabled, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, time on site, general location, device type). This uses cookies - see our Cookie Policy for details.

Why: To improve the website and understand what content is useful to visitors.
Legal basis: Your consent (via the cookie banner).
Retention: Per Google Analytics' standard retention settings (typically 14 months).

During an engagement

If you become a client, we will also collect and store tender documents you share with us, bid drafts and scoring notes, and any other commercial or procurement information you provide.

Why: To deliver the service you've contracted for.
Legal basis: Contractual obligation (your engagement letter with us).
Retention: We keep engagement documents for 6 years after the project ends (standard practice for business records), then securely delete them. If you request deletion sooner, we will honour that where legally possible.

3. How we store your data

Contact form data: Submitted via Netlify Forms, with encryption in transit. See Netlify's privacy policy.

Website analytics: Where enabled, processed by Google LLC via Google Analytics. See Google's privacy policy.

Engagement documents: Stored in Google Drive, access restricted to Brad Richards only. Files are encrypted at rest and in transit.

All third-party services used have adequate safeguards under UK GDPR.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except where you explicitly ask us to (e.g. forwarding a bid to a supplier on your behalf), where required by law (e.g. to HMRC for tax purposes, or a court order), or with service providers who process data on our behalf (Netlify, Google) under their own privacy terms.

We never sell or rent your data.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct inaccurate data, request deletion (subject to legal constraints - we may need to keep some records for tax/contract purposes), restrict processing, withdraw consent for analytics or marketing at any time, and receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email brad@procurementhelps.com with "Data Subject Request" in the subject line. We'll respond within 30 days.

6. Contact us

Email: brad@procurementhelps.com

If you believe we've mishandled your data and our response doesn't resolve it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services means you accept the updated notice.
